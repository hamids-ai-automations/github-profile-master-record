## 🎯 Executive Problem Statement

Standard Google Search Console (GSC) is great at reporting raw search metrics, but terrible at telling marketing heads and website owners **what to do next**:

1. **Deceptive Arithmetic Averages**: Averaging CTR percentages across queries produces mathematically flawed numbers that disguise revenue opportunities.
2. **Unexplained Traffic Collapses**: When organic traffic drops 30%, GSC displays a downward graph line without diagnosing whether the drop was caused by SERP competitor displacement, AI Overview cannibalization, seasonality, or a technical 404/noindex error.
3. **Silent Content Decay**: High-performing cornerstone articles slowly bleed rankings over 6–12 months without automated alerting.
4. **Keyword Cannibalization Waste**: Multiple URLs split clicks and impression share for the same keyword topic, depressing both URLs from entering the top 3.

**SearchPulse AI** solves this by converting raw Google Search Analytics API payloads into **prioritized, evidence-backed sprint tickets** that directly translate to organic traffic and revenue growth.

## 🚀 Core Features & Capabilities

### ⚡ Feature A: High-Precision Search Performance Dashboard
- **True Mathematical Aggregation**: Calculates aggregate CTR as $\frac{\sum \text{Clicks}}{\sum \text{Impressions}} \times 100$ rather than averaging percentages.
- **Impression-Weighted Average Position**: Ranks weighted by visual SERP exposure.
- **Period-over-Period Delta Analysis**: Real-time comparisons (Last 28 Days vs Prior 28 Days, or 90-Day windows) with absolute and percentage shifts.
- **Daily Time-Series Trajectory**: Visual trajectory mapping clicks against impression momentum.

### 🎯 Feature B: Keyword Opportunity Finder
- Compares real-world keyword CTR against an empirical **SERP Position Expected CTR curve**.
- Identifies striking-distance keywords (Positions 2 through 15) with high impressions (>100) and below-expected CTR.
- Computes estimated **Potential Clicks Unlocked**.
- Generates targeted recommendations (SERP title hook rewrites, FAQ/Review Schema injection, H2 heading additions, internal link equity transfers).

### 📉 Feature C: Organic Traffic Drop Investigator
- Diagnostic engine identifying pages, queries, devices, and countries with statistically significant drops ($\ge 20\%$).
- Categorizes probable root cause:
  - **Critical De-Indexation / 404 Error**: Clicks collapse to 0 with matching impression drop.
  - **Ranking Decline / Competitor Displacement**: Position drop $\ge 3.0$ ranks.
  - **Search Demand / Seasonality**: Stable position with contracting impressions.
  - **CTR Erosion (AI Overviews / SERP Features)**: Stable position & impressions with declining CTR.

### ⏳ Feature D: Content Decay Planner
- Identifies cornerstone URLs experiencing sustained month-over-month decay ($\ge 25\%$).
- Pinpoints the exact lost search queries driving the decline.
- Automatically outputs step-by-step content refresh blueprints.

### 🔀 Feature E: Keyword Cannibalization Detector
- Scans for queries where 2+ landing pages each receive $\ge 15\%$ impression share.
- Distinguishes destructive cannibalization (depressing striking-distance ranks) from healthy multi-page dominance.
- Recommends 301 consolidation, canonicalization, or intent differentiation.

### 📋 Feature F: SEO Action Center with ICE Prioritization
- Unified sprint backlog scored by **ICE (Impact, Confidence, Effort)** from 1 to 100.
- Classified into **P0 (Critical)**, **P1 (High)**, and **P2 (Medium)**.
- Every ticket includes a **prefilled, one-click WhatsApp consulting link** to discuss technical execution directly with **Muhammad Hamid**.

### 📄 Feature G: Instant Executive Reporting
- **Standalone Executive HTML Report**: One-click download of a high-converting, boardroom-ready SEO audit report with charts, diagnostics, and direct WhatsApp contact.
- **CSV Data Exporter**: Clean exports for queries and landing pages.

### ⚡ Instant Sandbox Demo Mode (Zero Credentials Required!)
- Anyone (recruiters, clients, or developers) can clone and run SearchPulse AI immediately without connecting a Google Cloud account.
- Ships with 90 days of realistic e-commerce and marketing SaaS Search Console fixtures (`example-store.com`).
